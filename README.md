# Competitor Pricing Notes — Q3 Research Pack

_Internal notes repo for market research. Shared read-only for analysis._

## Summary

This repo contains a working set of notes comparing public pricing pages
for three SaaS competitors, gathered for the Q3 planning review.

## Key findings

- Competitor A moved to usage-based pricing in June, dropping their entry
  tier by ~18%.
- Competitor B still uses flat per-seat pricing; no changes detected since
  January.
- Competitor C introduced an annual-commit discount (2 months free) that
  undercuts our current enterprise offer by roughly 15%.

## Methodology

Pricing pages were checked manually over the last two weeks. No automated
scraping was used. Numbers above are rounded for the summary; see the
(not-yet-published) spreadsheet for exact figures.

## Notes for next review

- Revisit Competitor A's usage tiers once their Q3 earnings call happens.
- Competitor C's annual discount might just be a launch promo — check back
  in 60 days before reacting.
